Privacy Notice: Sponsorship and Partnership Expression of Interest

Who is responsible for your information

The University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU), as host, and the International Council for Open and Distance Education (ICDE), as owner of the 31st ICDE World Conference, are joint personal information controllers for the information you submit through this form, under a Data Sharing Agreement. UPOU is your first point of contact. This notice covers this form only.

What we collect

Your organization's name, type, country, and website; the contact person's name, position, email address, and optional phone number; and your stated interests, preferred package, and any text you add.

Why we collect it, and our legal basis

To respond to your inquiry, discuss and prepare sponsorship or partnership agreements, allow ICDE to review them, and report on and audit sponsorships. We rely on our legitimate interests in organizing the conference (Section 12(f) of RA 10173) and, once you ask to proceed, on steps taken at your request before an agreement (Section 12(b)). We do not rely on your consent. We will not add you to newsletters or mailing lists without asking you separately.

Who receives it

Conference Organizing Committee members who handle sponsorship and partnerships; UPOU offices that support the conference, such as finance; ICDE (Oslo, Norway), to review and approve arrangements and for reporting and audit; Floq, the platform provider that hosts this form; and government bodies or auditors where required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Transfers outside the Philippines

Floq and ICDE may store or process your information outside the Philippines, including in Norway. UPOU and ICDE must protect it under the Philippine Data Privacy Act and, where it applies, the European Union's GDPR.

How long we keep it

If your expression of interest does not lead to an agreement, we delete it within 90 days after the conference ends. Records of executed agreements and related financial records are kept as required by Commission on Audit rules and University records policies. ICDE must delete or return personal data it receives from UPOU within 30 days after the data sharing arrangement ends. Any data files given to ICDE for future conferences contain organization-level information only.

How we protect it

UPOU and ICDE apply organizational, physical, and technical safeguards consistent with the University of the Philippines System Data Privacy Manual and the Data Sharing Agreement. If you give us personal information about someone else, please make sure they know and have seen this notice.

Your rights

You may ask to access or correct your information, object to its processing, request its erasure or blocking, claim damages for unlawful processing, and complain to the National Privacy Commission. Where the GDPR applies, you may have further rights, including complaining to your local data protection authority. UPOU and ICDE will coordinate their response to your request.
