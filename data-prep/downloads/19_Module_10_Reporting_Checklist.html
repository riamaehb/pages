LGU Administrative Reporting Checklist

Use for monthly, quarterly, or other recurring administrative reports. Adapt to your office's policies.

Before processing

  • ☐ Confirm the reporting question, period, audience, and indicator definitions.
  • ☐ Define what one record represents and which identifier(s) apply.
  • ☐ List sources, custodians, dates received, and versions.
  • ☐ Keep untouched copies of authorized source files.

During processing

  • ☐ Check required fields, duplicates, invalid values, and date formats.
  • ☐ Use approved reference lists and repeatable cleaning rules.
  • ☐ Review exceptions and record decisions in a cleaning log.
  • ☐ Distinguish new records from updates and legitimate repeat transactions.
  • ☐ Check matches and unmatched records before combining tables.

Before submitting

  • ☐ Reconcile record counts and status totals with the sources.
  • ☐ Verify numerator, denominator, reporting period, and any exclusions.
  • ☐ Note unresolved cases and interpretation limits.
  • ☐ Confirm reviewer approval and remove unauthorized personal details.

After submitting

  • ☐ Save the dated report, source register, cleaning log, and quality checks.
  • ☐ Record any changes to definitions or methodology.
  • ☐ Store and share files only in approved locations under office rules.

A repeated beneficiary ID does not necessarily mean a duplicate application. Check the unit of record before deduplicating.
