Data engineering problems

Data from different sources often requires cleaning, transformation, and integration before it can be used for analysis.

Fragmented

The same information lives in separate apps, spreadsheets, and documents that do not talk to each other.

records.xlsx · form responses · report.pdf

Inconsistent

One value written several ways breaks counts, joins, and comparisons.

BSCS · BS CS · B.S. Computer Science

Incomplete

Missing, outdated, or unverifiable fields, and no record of who changed what.

units: "" · −3 · ?

Unstructured

Useful facts sit inside narrative text, waiting to be extracted and checked.

"…enrollment reached 4,812 students…"

Much of our research is motivated by data management problems encountered in institutional information systems, particularly data quality, integration, and reuse.

Research areas

The lab's previous student activities focused on crowdsourcing and human computation, particularly the design of tasks through which people contribute to data collection and verification. That work continues as the lab expands into other areas of data engineering.

These areas are related. For example, LLM-assisted information extraction may also involve human verification, schema matching, and provenance tracking.

Research methods and evaluation

Students are expected to formulate a research question, develop an appropriate method or prototype, and evaluate the results against relevant baselines.

ProblemInconsistent data from several office spreadsheets
QuestionCan automated cleaning reduce manual effort without losing accuracy?
MethodBuild a cleaning assistant with rules, suggestions, and review
EvaluationCompare accuracy, time, and human effort against a manual baseline
FindingsWhat works, under which conditions, and with what limitations

Research opportunities

Students interested in the lab are welcome to discuss possible research topics, including their own proposals. Useful preparation includes:

  • Interest in a real data problem
  • Willingness to learn databases, data processing, and evaluation methods
  • Openness to finding out how well a proposed solution actually works

These are illustrative prototypes. The proposed projects would require further development and evaluation.
